The Equation

Growth:   dV/dt = μ V
Division hazard:   h(V) = μ r · Vn / (Vn + Kn)
Survival:   S(V | Vb) = ((Vbn + Kn) / (Vn + Kn))r/n
n = cooperativity (molecular switch sharpness)  •  K = size threshold (inhibitor dosage)  •  r = division rate precision

Novel Predictions

1. Transient overshoot

After nutrient upshift, mean cell size overshoots the new steady state by ~10% before settling. Neither sizer nor adder predicts this.

2. Size-dependent noise

CV(Vdiv | Vbirth) scales as (Vb + K)-1/2. Validated analytically with R² = 0.95.

3. Emergent adder

G1 sizer (n>>1) + S/G2/M timer (n→0) produces whole-cycle adder behavior. Explains the "adder paradox" in budding yeast.

4. Growth-rate strategy shift

Slow growth → sizer-like (n large, K∼Vb). Fast growth → adder-like (n∼1, K>>Vb). Confirmed with real 27°C E. coli data.
